How it works

1

Watch the sharp books and exchanges. Pinnacle, Circa, Betfair, BetCris and others take big bets from pros, so they move first when real news hits.

2

Check that they agree. One book moving alone is often noise. 3+ moving together is steam. A line moving against where most public bets are is reverse line movement.

3

Work out the fair price. Remove the bookmaker's cut and blend the sharp books. Worst case mode only shows bets that are +EV under every method.

4

Find the slow books. Any of the other 82 books still showing the old price is a bet with an edge. Edge = fair win chance × payout − 1.

5

Grade yourself on CLV. If you keep getting better prices than the closing line, you're doing it right, even when a bet loses.
